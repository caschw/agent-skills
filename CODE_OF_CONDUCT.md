# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best
for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/caschw). Maintainers will review
reports promptly and confidentially and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including enforcement guidelines, is
available at the link above and applies to this project.
